PANDUAN VIDEO ILUSTRASI AI - PERSONALITY EXPLORER: VIDEO INTERAKTIF AI
===================================================

Total 20 video. Simpan di folder "videos" dengan nama persis seperti di bawah.
Durasi saran: 4-8 detik per video (video akan berputar ulang otomatis / loop).
Rasio saran: 1:1 (persegi) agar serasi dengan gambar yang sudah ada. 16:9 juga bisa.

TIPS
- 15 video pilihan (A/B/C): gunakan gambar di folder "images" sebagai gambar awal
  (fitur image-to-video di Veo, Runway, Kling, Luma, Hailuo, dll). Karakter dan
  gaya gambar otomatis konsisten. Untuk q1-c dan q4-c yang berisi beberapa panel,
  video sebaiknya menceritakan urutan panel tersebut secara berurutan.
- 5 video soal: belum ada gambar awalnya. Buat dari teks (text-to-video) atau buat
  dulu satu gambar situasi, lalu ubah menjadi video. Sertakan "same character,
  same art style" agar tokoh sama dengan video pilihan.
- Video bersuara tidak wajib. Game memutar video tanpa suara dulu (aturan browser);
  siswa dapat menyalakan suara lewat kontrol video.
- Hindari teks/tulisan di dalam video, karena AI sering salah menulis.

GAYA UMUM (tempel di awal setiap prompt)
"2D cartoon animation, soft warm colors, Indonesian junior high school students in
white shirts and navy uniforms, same main character with short black hair, smooth
gentle motion, no text."

===================================================
VIDEO SOAL  (videos/qN-soal.mp4)
===================================================
q1-soal   Masuk Kelas Baru
  Prompt: The main student stands at the doorway of a new classroom, looking at
  classmates he does not know yet; the camera moves slowly in.

q2-soal   Tugas Kelompok
  Prompt: A teacher hands out group assignment papers in class; the main student
  receives a paper and looks at his classmates around the table.

q3-soal   Waktu Istirahat
  Prompt: A school bell rings; students in the classroom look up and start moving;
  the main student looks around deciding what to do.

q4-soal   Presentasi di Depan Kelas
  Prompt: The teacher calls a student's name; the main student looks up, holding
  notes, while classmates turn to look at him.

q5-soal   Waktu Luang Setelah Sekolah
  Prompt: The main student walks out of the school gate after class with a
  backpack, afternoon light, thinking about what to do next.

===================================================
VIDEO PILIHAN  (videos/qN-a|b|c.mp4)   <- gambar awal: images/qN-x.jpg
===================================================
q1-a  Siswa duduk tenang di bangku sambil mengamati teman-teman di kelas.
  Motion: he sits quietly, eyes slowly looking around the classroom.
q1-b  Siswa berdiri, melambaikan tangan, menyapa teman-teman.
  Motion: he waves and smiles, classmates turn and greet him back.
q1-c  Gambar atas mengamati, gambar bawah berkenalan.
  Motion: he first observes quietly, then waves and begins talking with a classmate.

q2-a  Siswa menulis di buku sendiri, teman-teman berdiskusi di meja yang sama.
  Motion: he writes in his notebook while others discuss beside him.
q2-b  Siswa berdiskusi dengan kelompok, menunjuk kertas, membagi tugas.
  Motion: he points at the paper, everyone nods and talks together.
q2-c  Siswa menulis bagiannya sendiri setelah berdiskusi.
  Motion: the group talks briefly, then he turns to write his own part.

q3-a  Siswa membaca buku sendirian saat istirahat.
  Motion: he turns a page of the book; trees sway outside the window.
q3-b  Siswa berjalan bersama teman-teman sambil mengobrol ke kantin.
  Motion: the group of friends walks and chats together toward the canteen.
q3-c  Siswa berdiri sambil berpikir, ragu pergi bersama teman atau tetap di kelas.
  Motion: he thinks with his hand on his chin, glancing from friends to the classroom.

q4-a  Siswa memegang catatan, bersiap sebelum presentasi.
  Motion: he looks over his notes, takes a calm breath, then looks up.
q4-b  Siswa langsung berbicara di depan kelas dengan gerakan tangan.
  Motion: he speaks confidently, gesturing with his hand while classmates listen.
q4-c  Empat panel: gugup di awal, lalu menyesuaikan diri dan mulai berbicara.
  Motion: he looks nervous, scratches his head, then relaxes and starts speaking.

q5-a  Siswa santai di bean bag bermain game sendirian di kamar.
  Motion: he plays a handheld game, thumbs moving, smiling slightly.
q5-b  Siswa bermain gitar dan bola bersama teman di taman.
  Motion: he strums the guitar while friends laugh and play with a ball nearby.
q5-c  Siswa memegang stik game, membayangkan bermain bersama teman dan membaca sendiri.
  Motion: he thinks, two thought bubbles appear: friends playing together, and
  himself relaxing alone with a book.
